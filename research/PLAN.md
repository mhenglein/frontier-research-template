# Research plan

This plan records priorities, not assignments. Current ownership belongs in
`STATE.md` and `research/registry.json`.

## Priority order

1. Make the exact problem and notation unambiguous.
2. Map existing results and their hypotheses.
3. Run the cheapest falsification tests for the most important assumptions.
4. Develop one end-to-end route with named obligations.
5. Obtain independent mathematical review and selective formal checks.
6. Reproduce the accepted evidence from a clean clone.

## Route template

For each route, record:

- `Axxx` and the exact target.
- Why this route could answer the problem.
- The next obligation that would distinguish it from alternatives.
- Dependencies on `Cxxx`, `Exxx`, or `Qxxx` records.
- A bound, resource limit, or stopping condition.
- What result would make the route `completed`, `blocked`, or `abandoned`.
