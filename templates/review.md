## Independence

Did the reviewer inherit the original argument, implementation, or test? State
what was independently reconstructed or checked, and list any shared premises.

## Scope checked

Describe the exact files, declarations, experiments, inputs, quantifiers,
hypotheses, imported results, and edge cases checked. Separate mathematical,
software or experiment, and formal checks.

## Findings

List the result of each check. Link counterexamples, receipts, logs, or notes.
Use `PASS`, `FAIL`, `PENDING`, `SKIPPED`, or `NOT RUN` where a check has a
bounded outcome. A timeout or incomplete search remains `UNKNOWN`.

## Outcome

Review outcome: accepted / accepted with obligations / needs correction / not
independent / inconclusive

State whether the evidence supports the record's current status. Do not promote
a claim to `established` or `refuted` from a green build alone.

## Limitations and remaining obligations

Name the parts not checked, the assumptions that remain open, and the one next
review or research question that matters most.
